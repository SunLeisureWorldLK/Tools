Day 01: Welcome to Thailand & Coral Island Meals: (--/L/--)

Day 02: Spiritual Vibes & Journey to Bangkok Meals: (B/--/--)

Enjoy a hearty breakfast before checking out. Seek blessings at the majestic Pattaya Big Buddha Temple, soaking in the serene atmosphere. Afterward, enjoy a comfortable private transfer to the bustling metropolis of Bangkok. By 14:00, check into your premium 3-star hotel (Picnic Hotel, Hotel De Bangkok, Tango Vibrant, or similar) for a peaceful overnight stay.

Day 03: Leisure & Enchanting River Cruise Meals: (B/--/D)

Start your day with breakfast and enjoy a free day to explore Bangkok's famous shopping havens and vibrant streets at your own pace. As the sun sets, embark on a magical Chao Phraya Cruise Dinner (Join Transfer). Indulge in a lavish buffet while gliding past Bangkok's illuminated landmarks. Overnight at the hotel.

Zoom:

Day 04: Thrills at Siam Amazing Park Meals: (B/L/--)

Fuel up with breakfast before heading to the ultimate playground—Siam Amazing Park! Enjoy a fun-filled day of thrilling rides and exciting water park splashes. A fantastic lunch is included to keep your energy high throughout the adventure. Return to your hotel for your final night in Bangkok.

Day 05: Farewell Thailand Meals: (B/--/--)

Savor your final breakfast in Bangkok. Spend your morning at leisure before a 12:00 PM checkout. A private transfer will whisk you away from your Bangkok hotel directly to Bangkok Airport for your safe journey home, carrying unforgettable memories. Tour Ends!
